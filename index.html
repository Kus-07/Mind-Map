Welcome to MindMate

MindMate is a practice space for stress and coping skills — inspired by chatbots such as Woebot. You can check in, reframe a stressful thought, build a coping plan, reflect on workload, practise a supportive conversation, or learn how stress works.

  • It is not a therapist or counsellor and cannot diagnose anything.
  • It is not for emergencies. If you might hurt yourself or are in danger, call Tele-MANAS 14416 or 112 now.
  • Powered by AI, no login needed. When you’re online, MindMate replies using AI (Claude). AI can make mistakes — use your own judgement. If the AI can’t be reached, guided built-in conversations and all exercises still work.
  • Your chat messages are not saved. Only if you allow it below, your check-ins and exercise ratings are remembered — in this browser only.

Get help now

You deserve support from a real person. If you are thinking about harming yourself, or feel unsafe:

If you are outside India, contact your local emergency number or a local crisis line.

About MindMate

What it is

A friendly practice space for stress and coping skills, inspired by chatbots such as Woebot. You can check in, reframe a stressful thought, build a coping plan, reflect on workload, practise a supportive conversation, learn how stress works, and try breathing, relaxation, grounding and journaling exercises.

How it learns

MindMate starts empty. It learns only from how you use it — your check-ins, the topics you mention, and how helpful you rate each exercise — and uses this to suggest what has worked for you before.

Your privacy

  • Your chat messages are not saved.
  • Check-ins, ratings and journal entries are kept only in this browser, and only if you allow it. You can switch this off, download it or delete it in 🔒 My data.
  • Your journal stays private unless you tap “Ask MindMate for a gentle reflection” on an entry.
  • When AI is on, your messages are sent to the AI service to create replies. Please don’t type names or personal details.

AI replies

MindMate replies using AI (Claude) when you are online — the badge at the top shows ● AI online. The AI also reads your check-ins (with your permission) to notice patterns and suggest exercises, and can reflect on a journal entry if you choose to share it. If the AI can’t be reached, it shows ○ Built-in mode and uses guided conversations instead. AI replies can be wrong — use your own judgement.

Please remember

MindMate is not a therapist or counsellor, cannot diagnose anything, and is not for emergencies. If you might hurt yourself or are in danger, call Tele-MANAS 14416 or 1-800-891-4416 (free, 24×7) or 112, and reach out to someone you trust.
